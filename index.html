§03 — about

Who's actually typing.

I'm Kevin. Computer engineering at Michigan, mostly pointed at robotics. I got here the boring way — I wanted a thing to exist, nobody was going to build it for me, so I learned KiCad.

Most of what's on this page started with someone saying "can you make something that does X." A handheld controller for teaching robot arms. A cockpit board for an electric race boat. An enclosure that survives a factory washdown. I usually take the whole thing — board, firmware, and the CAD — because handing a design off halfway is how you end up with a connector that doesn't fit.

Before any of it I made posters, which turned out to be the same job with different constraints.

If I'm not in the Robotics Building lab, try the poker room, the driving range, or the bouldering gym. Or just call.
